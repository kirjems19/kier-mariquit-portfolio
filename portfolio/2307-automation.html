Portfolio Project 01

BIR 2307 Automation

A workflow automation concept developed to reduce repetitive activities involved in request handling, document checking, tracking, and response coordination.

The Problem

Repetitive document-related requests can require multiple manual steps, including receiving requests, checking files, updating trackers, locating documents, and responding to requestors.

Repetitive Tracking

Request status needed to be monitored and updated consistently to determine which items had already been processed.

Document Checking

Supporting documents needed to be located and checked against the request before the next process step.

Response Handling

Request-related communication could involve repetitive email responses and status updates.

Multiple Platforms

The workflow involved information moving between forms, spreadsheets, cloud storage, scripts, and email.

The Solution

Connected workflow automation

I designed a workflow that connects the request, tracking, document, and communication stages into a more structured process.

My Role

From operational requirement to workflow

The project was developed from my understanding of the actual operational process and the repetitive activities involved in handling requests.

Process Understanding

I mapped the operational steps and identified activities that could be structured or automated.

Workflow Design

I structured the connection between request submission, tracking, document checking, and response handling.

Automation Logic

I used Apps Script and spreadsheet logic to support status checking and workflow triggers.

Operational Testing

The workflow was developed around real operational requirements rather than as a purely theoretical exercise.
