Portfolio Project 03

Pocket Locker

A functional personal finance tracking system I built to organize personal, business, and side finances in one connected workspace.

01 — Overview

One workspace for financial tracking

Pocket Locker was created to help me track different areas of my finances, including expenses, income, savings, borrowing, lending, and other transactions.

02 — Why I Built It

Built around the way I manage money

I initially built Pocket Locker because I wanted to track my finances across personal activities as well as business and side activities, while also keeping savings connected to the same tracking system.

As I continued developing it, I added ideas based on situations I personally encountered, including tracking people involved in shared or split expenses.

03 — Core Features

What Pocket Locker currently tracks

01

Expense Tracking

Records and organizes expenses through a dedicated input and tracking area.

02

Income & Savings

Keeps income and savings information connected to the overall financial tracking system.

03

Borrow & Lend

Tracks financial transactions involving money borrowed or lent to other people.

04

Payment Status

Supports tracking of partial, overdue, and updated payment situations within borrowing and lending records.

05

Shared Expenses

Includes ideas for tracking people involved in shared or split expenses based on real situations.

06

Financial Analytics

Provides an analytics area to help visualize and review financial information.

04 — Workflow

From transaction input to financial insight

The system brings different financial activities together so they can be recorded, organized, reviewed, and analyzed from one place.

01 — Record Enter an income, expense, savings activity, or other financial transaction.
02 — Organize Associate the transaction with the relevant category, person, or financial activity.
03 — Track Monitor outstanding, partial, overdue, or updated transactions where applicable.
04 — Review Use the dashboard to review the current financial information.
05 — Analyze Use the analytics area to understand financial activity.
06 — Improve Continue developing the system based on actual financial tracking needs.
